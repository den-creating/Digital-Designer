### 🎨 What I Do

- **UI/UX & Web Design:** Wireframing, interactive prototyping, and responsive web layouts in Figma.
- **Brand & Marketing Collateral:** Visual identities, ad creatives, presentations, and social graphics using Photoshop and Canva.
- **Generative AI Art & Assets:** Synthetic visual design, rapid ideation, and concept generation using cutting-edge AI image tools.
- **Visual Strategy:** Designing data-driven creatives optimized for conversion and cross-platform engagement.
